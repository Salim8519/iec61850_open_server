# 0063: Tidy up the single-line diagram

Date: 2026-10-14. Owner request: a smart "tidy up" in the SLD so a random diagram
gets a clear design like a real SLD. Specification: [SLD tidy](../../specs/sld-tidy.md).
Decision: [ADR-0040](../../decisions/ADR-0040-sld-tidy-up.md).

## Change

- `frontend/src/equipment/tidy/`:
  - `graph.ts`: wiring graph, groups, busbars and junction-aware distances.
  - `rows.ts`: top busbars, wire-distance rows and bar blocks.
  - `position.ts`: crossing reduction, weighted isotonic column fit and row heights.
  - `wires.ts`: wire sides and symbol turns.
  - `tidy.ts`: groups side by side, parking unwired items, anchoring and settle passes.
- `features/diagram/TidyButton.tsx` and `tidyCopy.ts`: **Tidy up** / **Tidy selection** in
  the Diagram toolbar, with a status line and a refit of the view; `diagram.css` styles
  the status line.
- `frontend/tsconfig.json`: `allowImportingTsExtensions` (see ADR-0040).
- `tests/frontend/sldTidy.test.ts` (TIDY-01..10) and a fixture,
  `tests/frontend/fixtures/model_substation_diagram.json`, generated by the existing SLD
  import from `scd/model_substation.scd`.

## Evidence

**Tests**
- `sldTidy.test.ts`: 10 passing. All frontend tests: 79 passing.
- `npm run build` (tsc and Vite) passes.

**Fuzz** (scratch script, not committed): 400 random diagrams of 2 to 60 items plus one of
2,000, with loops and a wire to a missing item.
- No overlaps and no changed wiring.
- 396 of 400 unchanged by a second press.
- 2,000 items take about 0.3 s per pass and about 1.1 s at worst.

**Browser** (isolated instance on port 8799 with a scratch data folder; the owner's
running session and autosave were not touched)
- The model substation diagram with every position scrambled, plus a relay panel and a
  push button, tidied in one press into:
  - MV board 1;
  - a 50 kV double busbar: BB1 and BB2 stacked, each bay's B1/B2 disconnectors joining
    at a centred tee, then breaker, CT and line disconnector in a straight column;
  - MV board 2;
  - the panel and button parked on the right.
- "All changes saved". A second press said "The diagram is already tidy". One Undo
  restored the scrambled drawing and Redo reapplied it. No console errors.
- A 5 px kink under one disconnector was found and fixed (junction rows finish under
  what feeds them) before the final check.

## Limitations

- Wires remain smooth-step lines, so a wire to an item in another column bends once.
- Arbitrary meshes are not guaranteed crossing-free. About 1% of random non-SLD wirings
  still shift a little on a second press.
- No bay or voltage-level labels.
- The owner's visual acceptance is pending.
