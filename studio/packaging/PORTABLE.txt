ScadaBench portable

1. Put this ScadaBench folder anywhere you can write (Desktop, Documents, a USB stick).
2. Start ScadaBench.exe. Nothing is installed in Windows.
3. Your projects and settings stay in the data folder next to ScadaBench.exe.
   Keep it when you copy or update the folder.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: choose More info, then Run anyway.
