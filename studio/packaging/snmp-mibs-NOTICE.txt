Standard SNMP MIB modules in ScadaBench

ScadaBench contains, unmodified, the standard MIB modules listed below so that it can show
SNMP names (for example IF-MIB::ifOperStatus) without extra downloads. They are code
components extracted from IETF RFCs, plus modules from the IANA registries:

  SNMPv2-SMI (RFC 2578), SNMPv2-TC (RFC 2579), SNMPv2-CONF (RFC 2580), SNMPv2-MIB (RFC 3418),
  RFC1155-SMI (RFC 1155), RFC-1212 (RFC 1212), RFC1213-MIB (RFC 1213), RFC-1215 (RFC 1215),
  IF-MIB (RFC 2863), IP-MIB (RFC 4293), TCP-MIB (RFC 4022), UDP-MIB (RFC 4113),
  INET-ADDRESS-MIB (RFC 4001), SNMP-FRAMEWORK-MIB (RFC 3411),
  SNMP-USER-BASED-SM-MIB (RFC 3414), SNMP-TARGET-MIB, SNMP-NOTIFICATION-MIB and
  SNMP-PROXY-MIB (RFC 3413), ENTITY-MIB and UUID-TC-MIB (RFC 6933),
  ENTITY-SENSOR-MIB (RFC 3433), HOST-RESOURCES-MIB and HOST-RESOURCES-TYPES (RFC 2790),
  UPS-MIB (RFC 1628), BRIDGE-MIB (RFC 4188), NTPv4-MIB (RFC 5907), SYSAPPL-MIB (RFC 2287),
  IANAifType-MIB, IANA-ADDRESS-FAMILY-NUMBERS-MIB and IANA-ENTITY-MIB (IANA,
  https://www.iana.org/assignments/).
