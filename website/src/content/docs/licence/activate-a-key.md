A lifetime license has no time limit. It works on one computer only.

## Buy a license

1. Press **Buy lifetime license**. It opens the secure purchase page in your browser.
2. Pay on that page. Your key appears right after payment, and it is also sent by email.

Payment happens on that page, not inside ScadaBench.

## Activate this computer

1. Open the activation screen, or **Settings**, then the **License** tab.
2. Type your key under **License key**. The key looks like `SUBS-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`.
3. Press **Activate this computer**. The page says **Activating…** while it works.
4. When it is done, the status reads **Lifetime license** and **Licensed to** shows your name.

You need internet for this step. If this computer has no internet, see the Offline activation page in this section.

## One computer only

- A license works on one computer and cannot be moved to another.
- Reinstalling Windows on the same computer is fine.
- A new computer needs its own license.

## Refunds

If you have not activated the license yet, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of purchase. Email **support@scadabench.com** with your license key. The key is deactivated on refund. Once a license is activated, it cannot be refunded.

The free trial lets you test ScadaBench first. Start it from the Free trial page.
