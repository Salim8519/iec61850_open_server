The free trial gives you every feature of ScadaBench for 7 days. You need no card and no payment details. One trial is allowed per computer.

## Start the trial

You need internet for this step. Once the trial has started, ScadaBench works offline.

1. Start ScadaBench. The activation screen opens.
2. Type your email address under **Your email address**.
3. Press **Send code**.
4. Check your inbox and your spam folder. The email has a 6-digit code. The code works for 15 minutes.
5. Type the code under **Code from the email**.
6. Press **Start free trial**. The page says **Starting trial…** while it works.

Your email is only used to allow one free trial per person.

The first screen may also show a button named **Start 7-day free trial**. It starts the same trial.

## See how many days are left

Open **Settings**, then the **License** tab. The status reads, for example:

- **Free trial: 5 days left**

When 2 days or fewer are left, the status turns into a warning. Buy a lifetime license before the trial ends to keep working without a break.

## Check the date online

Press **Check online now** to confirm the date with the license server once. Your trial continues right away.

## When the trial ends

ScadaBench stops working until you activate a license. Your projects are safe. They stay in the data folder and open again as soon as ScadaBench is activated.
